How Plotlift works

Turn a picture of a graph back into numbers, the way g3data does.

Workflow

  1. Open a plot. An image file, a PDF page, a pasted screenshot, or a file dropped on the stage. Each one opens in its own tab.
  2. Calibrate. Place X1 and X2 on two known points of the X axis, and Y1 and Y2 on the Y axis, then type their values. Pick Linear or Log for each axis. Rotated or skewed axes work too.
  3. Mark points. Click on the data. The magnifier shows the pixels under the cursor; drag a point to adjust it. Or use Auto-detect: click the data to pick its colour, drag a box around the plot area, and add the points it finds.
  4. Export. Choose the order, separator and error columns, then copy the text or save a .txt or .csv file.

About the numbers

Values come from a full two-vector calibration: the X value is read along the X1→X2 line and the Y value along the Y1→Y2 line, so the axes need not be perpendicular or level. On a log axis the interpolation runs in log₁₀ space. The optional dx and dy columns propagate the click uncertainty (half an image pixel by default) through the calibration. Projects save the image, axes and every series in one .json file you can reopen later.
